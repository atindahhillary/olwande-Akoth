Chapter one. The why

Every safe space starts with someone who refuses to look away

Olwande is a multi-disciplinary Social Impact Advocate, PR professional, Eco-Fashion Designer and Programs Manager committed to mental health advocacy, women's empowerment and community-centred development. She is known for creating safe and inclusive spaces, leading transformative wellness programmes, and harnessing fashion, digital storytelling and grassroots mobilisation as tools for lasting social change. She brings a rare blend of strategic program leadership and creative advocacy, working across Kenyan organisations to bridge the gap between lived community experience and impactful institutional change.

Chapter two. In the field

Long before press releases, there were school assemblies

Before the titles and the platforms, the work looked like this: standing in a schoolyard, microphone in hand, asking a few hundred teenagers how they are really doing. Olwande's mental health advocacy started at ground level, meeting young people where they already are and making conversations about wellness, identity and emotional literacy feel normal rather than clinical.

Chapter three. Programs manager
HUGs Organization, since March 2024

Turning a mission worth hashtagging into a working machine

As Programs Manager, Olwande leads national mental health and community empowerment programmes with a particular focus on women's wellness and psychological safety. That means designing corporate wellness webinars and grassroots activations in the same week, then holding both to the same standard.

  • Curates women-only wellness events: picnics, dance nights and healing circles built to nurture mental resilience and peer belonging.
  • Facilitates cross-sector partnerships and mobilises community resources from design through evaluation.
  • Oversees strategic planning, monitoring and evaluation so impact is measured, not assumed.
Chapter four. Public advocacy

If the message matters, take it to where people already are

Some of the most effective advocacy does not happen in a boardroom. It happens on a Nairobi CBD pavement, banner in hand, briefing a partner organisation between conversations with strangers on their lunch break. Olwande's PR instincts translate causes into language passersby will actually stop for.

Chapter five. Hands-on impact
PR & Communications Officer, Octopizzo Foundation, since Nov 2025

Impact is also a box of sanitary pads and a working water filter

Alongside her HUGs role, Olwande supports Octopizzo Foundation's communications strategy for community development, sustainability and youth empowerment. She helped tell the story behind the Clean Energy Kitchen Initiative, a circular economy innovation that converts sugarcane husks into clean cooking fuel while distributing water filters to underserved households, reducing indoor air pollution and improving access to safe drinking water.

The rest of the work is less glamorous and just as necessary: sorting donated goods, restocking supply tables, and showing up at shelters after dark.

Chapter six. Creativity as advocacy

Fashion, a podcast mic and a conversation Kenya keeps needing to have

Off the programme calendar, Olwande designs upcycled, eco-conscious fashion pieces that turn reclaimed materials into wearable art, and curates bespoke styling experiences built around confidence and self-expression. As a digital creator, she makes content exploring Kenyan culture, modern womanhood, relationships and emotional wellness, bridging online influence with on-the-ground community conversations.

"Driven by purpose. Rooted in community. Powered by creativity."

By day, she also closes deals
